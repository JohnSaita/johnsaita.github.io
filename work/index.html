Transmission Risk Map

A global web map of transmission risk for seven mosquito-borne diseases, from whole countries down to individual districts.

Country → state → district, automatically

Zoom in and the map switches admin level on its own, showing exactly one level at a time so it stays readable and fast.

  • Three zoom levels. Countries when zoomed out, states in the middle, districts up close. Districts load per country only when needed.
  • Parent-region fallback. A district without data takes its state's value; a state without data takes its country's. Inherited values are hatched so they're never mistaken for real ones.
  • Built for phones. Country search, "my location" with the local risk level, and terrain layers, all usable on a 400px screen.
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Mapbox GL JS
  • geoBoundaries

All risk values in this demo are synthetic, generated for demonstration. They are not real risk estimates.

Case study

Benzene exposure in Kent and Medway, 2023

Which parts of Kent and Medway carry the most background benzene, and how many residents live there? Modelled concentrations, Census 2021 population and emission sources, combined in a rerunnable pipeline.

  • Well below the limit. The highest 1 km cell reaches 0.63 µg/m³, about one-eighth of the UK annual objective of 5 µg/m³.
  • The Thames side carries the burden. 337,566 residents (18.2%) live at or above 0.50 µg/m³, and 92% of them are in Medway, Dartford and Gravesham.
  • Big emitters don't set the pattern. 88 sites released 12.3 tonnes in 2023, yet emission size and local concentration are almost unrelated (Spearman ρ = 0.11).
